Before you use this result

    What this checks

    Outside boundary only. Thickness, holes, bevels, underside, squareness, and individual feature measurements need separate inspection.

    FIXED CAMERA / ONE-TIME SETUP

    Give the camera a sense of scale.

    1. Lock the phone above a dark matte surface. Use the same camera, orientation, resolution, and selected zoom for every shot.
    2. Place a plain, accurately measured rectangle at the same height as the plywood’s top face. A rectangular plywood offcut of the same thickness works. Use a large rectangle near the inspection area.
    3. Photograph it, then tap its corners in order: top left → top right → bottom right → bottom left. Enter its measured width and height.
    4. Save, then remove the rectangle. Recalibrate if anything moves, the lens/zoom changes, or the part’s thickness changes. Autofocus and lens distortion can still affect measurements.
