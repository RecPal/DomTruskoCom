Featured Project

IIoT Smart Manufacturing Test Cell

Manufacturing · Electrical Engineering · Data Visualization · Animation

Developed for the Society of Manufacturing Engineers and Manufacturing Education Alliance, our capstone project involved designing and building an educational manufacturing test cell. Using donated industrial hardware, we spent a year integrating robots, conveyors, sensors, and controls into a complete system. My work focused on electrical design, PLC programming, SCADA visualization, and SQL integration, alongside producing all animation and video editing. The completed platform was handed off with documentation and educational materials, allowing it to be reproduced for trade schools and technical programs.
